# Discount Margin Valuation of a Floating-Rate Security

An Excel-based fixed-income modelling exercise focused on the valuation of a floating-rate security and calculation of its **discount margin (DM)**.

Discount margin is the spread over a reference-rate curve that reconciles the present value of projected floating-rate cash flows with the security's observed market price, subject to the model's conventions and assumptions.

## Project Focus

- Floating-rate security cash-flow analysis
- Reference-rate and spread assumptions
- Discount-margin calculation
- Present-value-based valuation
- Sensitivity of valuation to discount margin

## How to Use

1. Download and open the workbook in Microsoft Excel or a compatible spreadsheet application.
2. Review the input cells and conventions documented in the workbook.
3. Adjust only designated market-price, reference-rate, cash-flow, and spread inputs.
4. Review the resulting valuation and discount-margin calculation.
5. Keep formula cells intact and confirm automatic spreadsheet recalculation.

## Fixed-Income Context

Floating-rate instruments generally reset coupons periodically against a reference rate. Their value depends on projected coupons, reset conventions, payment dates, principal repayment, credit/liquidity spread, and discounting assumptions.

## Limitations

The workbook's conventions, data, and formula structure govern its outputs. The calculated discount margin is not a universal market quote unless instrument terms, curve, day-count, compounding, reset schedule, and settlement assumptions match the relevant market convention.
